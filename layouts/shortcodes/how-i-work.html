How I work

  1. We talk. You tell me what is wrong or what you need. The first call is free, and I will say so if I am not the right person for it.
  2. You get a written scope and a fixed price. What I will deliver, how we will know it is done, and what it costs, before any work or access.
  3. I build it and hand it over. You see progress along the way, and at the end your team gets everything it needs to run it without me.
