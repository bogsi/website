_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

This page explains what personal data roussev.dev collects, why, where it is
kept, for how long, and what you can ask me to do with it.

## Who is responsible

The controller is **Bogomil Roussev**, Sofia, Bulgaria, acting as an
individual. For anything about your data, write to
[bogomil.roussev@gmail.com](mailto:bogomil.roussev@gmail.com).

## The inquiry form

**What:** the details you type into the [inquiry form](/order/): name, email
address, company, the service you are interested in, your project
description, budget and deadline (the last three are optional). I also keep
when the inquiry arrived, its status, my internal notes and a record of the
emails sent about it.

**Why:** to answer your inquiry, prepare an offer and, if we work together,
deliver the work. The legal basis is steps taken at your request before
entering into a contract, and the contract itself (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

**Emails:** sending the form triggers two emails: one to me with your
details, and a short confirmation to you that repeats none of what you typed.
When I change the status of your inquiry you get a short update. Emails are
sent through [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).

**Where:** in a database on Cloudflare (D1), reachable only by me through a
login protected by Cloudflare Access.

**How long:**

- Open inquiries and projects: as long as we are in contact.
- Closed ones (completed or declined): **deleted automatically 24 months after
  the last change**, together with their history and emails.
- Encrypted backups of the database: kept for **30 days**, then deleted.
- Copies of the notification emails in my mailbox: deleted on request.

## Bot protection and security

The form uses [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
to tell people from bots, and Cloudflare filters abusive traffic for the whole
site. To do this Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address
and browser signals. Server logs keep only technical data and an order number,
for at most a few days. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in keeping
the site and its inbox free of abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)).

## Analytics and cookies

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** counts page views without cookies and without
  identifying you.
- **Google Analytics 4** runs only **if you accept** it in the cookie banner
  (Art. 6(1)(a)). It is loaded through Cloudflare Zaraz from Cloudflare's
  servers, with your IP address shortened before it reaches Google, and no
  advertising features. If you accept, cookies recognise your returning
  visits for up to 12 months. Data is kept in Google Analytics for 14 months.
- Your choice itself is stored in a small cookie so the banner does not appear
  on every page.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at
the bottom of every page.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area.
Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is
certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. I do
not sell your data, and I make no automated decisions about you.

## Your rights

You can ask me to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it,
restrict how I use it, or send it to you in a portable format, and you can
object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to
[bogomil.roussev@gmail.com](mailto:bogomil.roussev@gmail.com); I answer within
one month. Deleting an inquiry removes it, its history and its emails at once.

You can also complain to the Bulgarian data protection authority, the
[Commission for Personal Data Protection](https://www.cpdp.bg/en/) (КЗЛД), or
to the authority where you live.

## Changes
