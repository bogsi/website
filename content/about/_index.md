I'm Bogomil Roussev, a DevOps and platform engineer in Sofia. I work on AWS
every day: Terraform, CI/CD, Kubernetes, cost and security. I also take on a
small number of fixed-price projects for other teams, which is what this site
is for.

I started on the hardware side: enterprise servers, storage and networks in
data centres, and support from the first call up to architecture. That is still
how I look at the cloud. Things fail in boring ways, somebody has to be able to
fix them at night, and the bill is part of the design.

## What I work with

